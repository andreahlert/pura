        Frequently asked questions

        Everything you need to know about plans, billing, and cancellation.

        Can I change plans later? Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time right from your dashboard. Changes are prorated and the amount is adjusted automatically on your next invoice. How does the annual discount work? When you choose annual billing you pay for 10 months and get 2 free, a 20% savings compared to the monthly plan. You are charged once per year. Which payment methods are accepted? We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), ACH bank transfer, and wire transfer for annual plans. Invoices are issued automatically. Is there a trial period? The Free plan is free forever, no credit card required. For the Pro and Team plans, we offer a 14-day trial with all features unlocked. Can I cancel anytime? Yes. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and your data stays available for export for 30 days.

        Prices in US dollars (USD), taxes not included. * Price per month on annual billing, charged once per year. Need something tailored to you? Talk to our sales team.
